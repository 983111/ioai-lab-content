# IOAI 2024 Tasks

The first International Olympiad in Artificial Intelligence took place in Bulgaria in August 2024. Its programme included a Scientific Round and a Practical Team Challenge.

## Rounds

### Scientific Round
- **At-home stage:** machine learning, natural language processing, and computer vision tasks, released for work over approximately one month.
- **On-site stage:** three tasks covering NLP, ML, and computer vision, completed during an eight-hour contest.

### Practical Team Challenge
Teams used generative AI tools to create an album cover and a music video for the supplied song.
